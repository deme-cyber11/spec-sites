Common Questions

Still wondering about something? Call (615) 414-3727 and ask.

Do you give free estimates?
Yes. Call (615) 414-3727 or send the form, and TKO sets a time to look at the floor.
Why do you grind the floor first?
Grinding and repair give the coating clean, open concrete to bond to. Every TKO floor starts there.
What kinds of floors do you install?
Garage floor coatings, epoxy with flake or quartz, urethane cement for kitchens, breweries and food plants, polished concrete, and concrete repair.
Do you work on commercial floors?
Yes. TKO floors are in factories, warehouses, offices, restaurants, a church and a brewery around Nashville.
