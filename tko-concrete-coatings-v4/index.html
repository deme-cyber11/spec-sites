Problems We Solve

Instead of putting another coating over a failing floor, TKO finds the cause and fixes it first. Then the new floor goes down.

Describe your floor

  • Failed or peeling coatings

    Delaminated epoxy comes off down to sound concrete before anything new goes down.

  • Moisture under the floor

    Moisture vapor is found and corrected so the new system does not fail the same way.

  • Cracked and spalled concrete

    Cracks, spalls and uneven slabs are repaired and leveled first.

  • Thermal shock and washdowns

    Urethane cement handles hot water, steam cleaning and constant moisture.

  • Forklift and impact wear

    Floors built for heavy equipment, dropped tools and daily forklift traffic.

  • Chemicals, oils and food acids

    Surfaces that resist cleaning chemicals, grease and food acids.

Questions

What Facility Managers Ask

Something else on your mind? Call (615) 414-3727.

How long does urethane cement flooring last?
When professionally installed and properly maintained, urethane cement can last 15 to 25 years or more, even in demanding food processing plants.
Is urethane cement USDA and FDA compliant?
The USDA and FDA do not approve flooring products directly. Urethane cement is widely used in USDA and FDA regulated plants because it meets the performance those facilities need.
Can you work around our production schedule?
Yes. TKO plans the work around facility shutdowns and has finished emergency repairs before production resumed.
How long does polished concrete last?
When professionally installed and properly maintained, a polished concrete floor can last 20 years or more.
Do you also do garage floors?
Yes. TKO installs residential garage coatings with the same industrial grade systems it uses on commercial floors.
Is the consultation free?
Yes. TKO looks at the slab, traffic, moisture and chemical exposure, then recommends the right system at no cost.
