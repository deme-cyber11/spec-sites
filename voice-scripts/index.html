Recording tips before you start
  • Quiet room, no background noise. Close doors, turn off fans.
  • Phone Voice Memos works fine — export as .m4a or .wav. No special app needed.
  • Read naturally. Normal pace, not radio voice. Pauses are good.
  • Each section is labeled. Record straight through — do not stop and re-read individual lines.
  • Aim for 10 minutes per script. ElevenLabs Instant Clone needs 1 min minimum; 10 min gives high quality. 30 min total unlocks Professional Voice Clone.
  • Upload at elevenlabs.io → Voices → Add Voice → Instant Voice Clone.
