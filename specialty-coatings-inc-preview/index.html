Questions

Floor Coating Questions Answered

Something else on your mind? Call (615) 591-9491.

Can existing concrete flooring be polished?

In many cases, existing concrete floors are excellent candidates for polishing.

When can a new concrete floor be acid stained?

It is best to wait at least 28 days up to 60 days after pouring a slab before finishing it.

Why use urethane as a topcoat over epoxy?

Urethanes are UV stable, so they will not yellow or amber over time, and they have up to three times better abrasion resistance than epoxy.

Where does Specialty Coatings work?
