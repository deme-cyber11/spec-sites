Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do you come to me, or do I drop the car off?

Both. We're fully mobile across Greater Austin — driveway, office lot, or apartment garage — and we bring our own water and power. Prefer to drop off? The in-shop bay is open too.

How much does a detail cost in Austin?

Interior details start at $179, exterior at $149, full details at $279, and ceramic/paint correction at $899 — pricing scales with vehicle size. Use the instant quote tool for an exact range in seconds.

Do you really do both interior and exterior?

Yes — that's the whole point of ATX. Our Full Detail covers a complete interior deep-clean and an exterior finish in one appointment, done to the same standard.

Can you get out pet hair and bad odors?

Absolutely — it's our specialty. Pet hair removal is a $49 add-on and ozone/odor treatment is $79. We get out what other shops give up on.

How long does a detail take?

An exterior or interior detail typically runs 1.5–3 hours; a full detail or ceramic coating longer. We'll give you a clear time window when you book.

Is there a deposit to book?

No deposit required to reserve your spot. You get a firm price up front and only pay when the work's done and you're happy.
