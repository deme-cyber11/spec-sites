Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we hear every week

Most residential septic tanks should be pumped every 3–5 years depending on household size and usage. A family of four with a 1,000-gallon tank typically needs service every 3 years. We'll inspect and give you a personalized schedule at no extra charge.
Residential pump-outs in the Lafayette area typically run $350–$550 for standard 1,000–1,500 gallon tanks. Larger tanks and difficult access may affect the final price. We provide a free, written estimate before any work begins — the price we quote is the price you pay.
Yes — Louisiana law requires a permit from the Louisiana Department of Health for most septic system installations, replacements, and major repairs. We handle all permitting paperwork on your behalf as part of our service. You don't need to deal with any government offices.
Call us immediately if you notice: sewage backing up into drains or toilets, a strong sewage odor inside or outside your home, unusually soggy or bright green patches over your drain field, or gurgling sounds from your plumbing. These are signs of a full tank or system failure — don't wait.
A standard residential pump-out takes 1–2 hours from arrival to completion, including inspection of baffles and access points. We leave your yard the way we found it — no mess, no damage to your lawn or landscaping.
Yes. For repairs and replacements over $500 we offer 0% financing for 12 months through GreenSky. Apply in minutes — most approvals are instant. Our technicians can walk you through the application on-site the day of your estimate.
Never flush wipes (even ones labeled "flushable"), feminine hygiene products, cooking grease, coffee grounds, or harsh chemical drain cleaners. These kill the beneficial bacteria that make your septic tank work and will accelerate system failure.
A properly maintained concrete septic tank can last 40+ years. Plastic and fiberglass tanks typically last 30+ years. Drain fields last 20–30 years on average. Regular pumping every 3–5 years is the single biggest factor in system longevity — and the cheapest insurance against a $10,000+ replacement.
